Mode & transport

Manual — fire Next stage (or Space) as the music reaches each section. The clock glides to that stage's time over its travel seconds, then holds. It never moves on its own.

Stages of the music

stageclocktravel scue
Clock is where the night stands when the stage lands (23:00 → 07:00). Travel is the glide length in manual mode, in real seconds. Cue is the moment in the track (m:ss) where the stage begins in track mode. Edits save in this browser.
